### CHEM-000473: Grape-derived dimethyl-trisulfide precursors in wine
- **Subfield:** Round 6
- **Open:** Identify grape-derived compounds required for substantial DMTS formation during aging. Establish their contribution independently of methanethiol and the sake precursor DMTS-P1.
- **Status:** The 2017 study found that dead yeast in synthetic must did not reproduce grape-juice behavior, while known precursors explained only a minor contribution. The operative grape-derived structures remained unidentified.
- **Type:** experimental
- **Why:** Connects grape and must composition to delayed sulfur defects.
- **Key works:** N. Nishibori, A. Kuroda, O. Yamada, and N. Goto-Yamamoto (2017), “Factors Affecting Dimethyl Trisulfide Formation in Wine,” [DOI: 10.3136/fstr.23.241](https://doi.org/10.3136/fstr.23.241).
- **Related:** Plant biology—nonvolatile sulfur metabolites in fruit.
